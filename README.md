Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261118** — *Sum up 18 November's
world news in one image*. A newspaper-front-page composition that tells the
day's biggest stories at a glance, with one short headline per panel.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) deterministically
copies the pre-rendered image `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`), creating the output
directory if needed. Running it twice produces byte-identical output.

- **Gulf escalation (lead story)** — Iran's IRGC launched drones at a US
  military radar at Sheikh Isa Air Base, Bahrain (3rd attack in 5 days);
  Bahrain/US say all drones were intercepted, no damage or injuries. US
  fighter jets struck an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island in
  response. Overnight into 18 Nov, Kuwait's air defences intercepted hostile
  drones and Bahrain issued fresh threat alerts, then an all-clear early

  with Flamingo cruise missiles (night of 16–17 Nov, second day of strikes);
  Russia pounds Kyiv bridges.
- **Asia diplomacy** — Trump arrives in Manila for Quad talks ahead of a
  China visit (may meet Putin at APEC in Shenzhen); Xi meets Canada's Carney

- **Britain** — UK sanctions Israeli settlement firms over West Bank
  settlements and a Gaza aid-blocking group.
- **Bottom strip** — Markets: Dow plunged 1,000+ points intraday Tuesday;
  Nvidia revenue up 103% year-on-year but CEO Jensen Huang will not return
  (affair with suspected Chinese spy); Cyclone Akvan intensifying over the
  Bay of Bengal; US loses measles elimination status amid its worst outbreak
  since 1991.
